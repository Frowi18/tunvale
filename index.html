Music player for Windows

Your music.
Your look.

Tunvale plays your own library and free online music in a clean, fast interface you can make your own - themes, backgrounds, equalizer, friends and shared playlists.

Download for Windows See features
Windows 10 / 11 · 64-bit · Free

Everything in one player

Built for people who listen a lot and like things to look right.

Your library

Add files and folders - MP3, FLAC, WAV, M4A, OGG and more. Covers and tags are read automatically.

Free online music

Search and play openly licensed music from several free catalogs right inside the app.

Playlists & folders

Playlists, folders, liked songs, drag and drop. Share playlists with friends.

Equalizer & effects

Classic sliders or a smooth curve, presets and a large set of sound effects.

Themes & backgrounds

Any accent color, gradients, pictures, seasonal backgrounds or your own image.

Friends & profiles

Sign in with Google, find friends, see what they listen to, public profiles.

Devices & sync

Playlists and settings follow your account. Control playback from another device.

Game overlay & mini player

Control music over games, compact mini player, global hotkeys, tray mode.

8 languages

English, Ukrainian, Russian, Kazakh, German, Chinese, Japanese and Polish.
